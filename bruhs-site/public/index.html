Seven apps' worth of work, one window.

Every part of bruhs shares one agent and one set of data on your machine. A meeting becomes tasks, a task goes to Claude, and your calendar makes room to review what it did.

Your coding agents, in the same room as everything else.

Run Claude Code and Codex in threads, with a terminal one keystroke away.

  • Follow every tool call and the diff from each turn.
  • Rewind an agent's worktree to an earlier turn without touching your branch.
  • Redirect Claude mid-reply, or queue your next message.

Tasks that know what's blocked and who you're waiting on.

Today puts your schedule, focus queue, overdue work and meeting decisions in one view.

  • Set priorities, estimates and due dates.
  • Link dependencies so blocked work stands out.
  • Plan in cycles, and make tasks repeat.

Your estimates turn into protected time.

“Plan my day” proposes focus blocks around your meetings and shows the whole change before anything moves.

  • Share booking links that respect your minimum notice and buffers.
  • Choose which copy to keep when Google and bruhs disagree.

Meetings that take their own notes, without a bot in the room.

bruhs records your mic and system audio, and Whisper transcribes it on your Mac. The audio never leaves your machine.

  • See who said what, with speaker labels.
  • Get a summary with decisions and action items.
  • Send action items straight to Tasks.

A CRM you never have to update.

People fill in from your meetings and the conversations you import.

  • Import from email, Slack, Discord, iMessage and X.
  • Keep commitments, reminders and private notes for each person.
  • Get a suggested follow-up before it slips.

Notes your agent can read.

Markdown docs, meeting notes and web clips live together, local and searchable.

  • Tag documents and connect them to your work.
  • Clip web pages and keep their source.
  • Add any doc or clip to a thread as context.

Money and health, kept with the rest of your day.

A local ledger and health log. Your agent sees them only after you opt in.

  • Track accounts, budgets, income and holdings, or import a CSV.
  • Log workouts, sleep, recovery and habits.
  • Keep exact amounts behind a second opt-in.

Bring your own sub.

bruhs runs the Claude Code and Codex CLIs you already have, on the subscriptions you already pay for. It never resells tokens or asks for an API key.

  • Claude Code$ claude auth login
  • Codex$ codex login
  • Choose Claude or Codex for each thread.
  • Keep Codex read-only until you allow edits.

Stays on your machine.

Your data lives in your browser's storage, or in the app's folder on your Mac.

On your device
Threads, tasks, notes and people. There's no bruhs cloud or sync server.
Meeting audio and transcripts. Whisper transcribes on your Mac, and no bot joins the call.
Money and health. Your agent can't see them until you opt in, and exact amounts need a second opt-in.
Online only when you ask
Messages to Claude or Codex. They go to Anthropic or OpenAI through your own login.
Google Calendar. Read-only, and only if you connect it.
