Privacy

This page covers the bruhs.ai website and its waitlist. The bruhs app itself keeps your data on your device. The privacy section of the home page lists what goes online.

What the waitlist stores

  • Your email address.
  • When you joined.
  • Which version of the signup wording you agreed to.

That's all. No name, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels.

Spam protection

To limit signups per network, the site keeps a one-way hash of your IP address for one hour, then it expires. We don't store the raw address.

What we use it for

We'll email you once, when bruhs is ready for you, and that email will have a one-click unsubscribe. We don't sell your address or share it for marketing.

Who processes it

The site is hosted on Vercel. Waitlist entries are stored in Upstash Redis. Both act as processors on our behalf.

Removing yourself

Email privacy@bruhs.ai from the address you signed up with, and we'll delete the entry.
